Manufacturing Systems · Research Prototype

From Fault to Action

A prototype exploring what happens after a manufacturing abnormality becomes visible. I designed a workflow that connects recurring faults with ownership, containment, root-cause investigation, countermeasures, and effectiveness verification. Shifting the focus from simply seeing problems to learning from and resolving them.

Lean Systems · Smart Manufacturing Concept

Smart MIFC

An exploration of how the traditional Material and Information Flow Charts can evolve for smart manufacturing.The concept adds digital information, decision points, and time awareness. Making it easier to understand not only how material and information move, but when information becomes available, when decisions must be made, and how those decisions affect production planning and performance.
