From Fault to Action

Moving from visible manufacturing faults to structured follow-through.

View demo · link pending Full details · link pending

Project summary

A research prototype that connects visible manufacturing abnormalities to ownership, containment, investigation, countermeasures, and verification—moving beyond detection toward learning and resolution.

This is an evolving concept summary. Add validated outcomes and live links as the work develops.

Problem Statement

Detecting a fault is only the beginning of problem solving. Without a consistent path to assign responsibility, contain the issue, investigate causes, and verify countermeasures, recurring abnormalities can remain unresolved.

Approach

Explore a human-centered workflow that follows an abnormality from first signal through resolution, while making ownership and learning explicit at each handoff.

Scope

A research prototype for an abnormality-management workflow. This is an exploration of process and information design rather than a claim about production deployment.

Actions

  1. Map the stages from fault detection to verification.
  2. Define ownership and key information needed at each stage.
  3. Explore containment, root-cause investigation, and countermeasure tracking.
  4. Design ways to capture effectiveness checks and recurring issues.

Impact

Intended value: stronger follow-through and better visibility into how issues are resolved. No time savings or quality improvements have been validated or reported here.

Lessons Learned

Working reflection: a useful digital workflow should reinforce disciplined problem solving rather than substitute for it. Add lessons from experiments and feedback as the prototype evolves.

Next Steps

  • Refine workflow steps and decision criteria.
  • Create or extend a prototype demonstration.
  • Test the flow using a representative scenario.
  • Document feedback and effectiveness measures to evaluate.
